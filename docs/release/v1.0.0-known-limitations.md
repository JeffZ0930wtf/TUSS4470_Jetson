# V1.0.0 known limitations

## Document overview

This document defines the limitations that remain accepted in the TUSS4470
acquisition module V1.0.0. It prevents bounded experimental evidence from
being interpreted as product-ready or calibrated performance. It complements
the V1 acceptance summary and is the current authority when an archived M0–M6
record discusses deferred work.

## Timing remains uncalibrated

Captures retain `TIMING_UNCALIBRATED`. Calibrated ultrasonic TOF, absolute
IO/event timing, IO_MODE 2 non-overlap, and sync-to-ADC offset still require
external timing instruments. Raw sample indices and nominal tick values are
available, but V1 must not present them as a calibrated physical time axis.

## Transport diagnostic CRC is not a content fingerprint

The exposed transport/inner-frame CRC diagnostic is calculated over an
already CRC-protected USAC frame and therefore has a fixed valid-codeword
residue. Native frame CRC validation remains effective, and terminal decision
SHA-256 plus raw-byte comparisons protect the accepted delivery checks. The
diagnostic field itself must not be used to distinguish capture contents.

## Lease and scheduling are first-version mechanisms

The accepted 10000 ms lease is an accommodation for synchronous storage and
confirmation latency, not a product default or a root-cause fix. It can delay
firmware auto-stop for roughly ten seconds after controller loss. Lease renewal
must eventually be decoupled from blocking storage work, or supplied by an
equivalently independent high-priority path.

At the 100 ms throughput ceiling and in one accepted 200 ms long run, the
device recorded explicit missed schedule opportunities while still completing
every requested capture. V1 therefore does not claim lossless 5 Hz or 10 Hz
long-term scheduling, although no acquired/committed frame was silently lost
in the accepted runs.

## Reconnect hardening remains incomplete

A deliberately disrupted stale Bridge session required a Core restart during
pre-V1 validation. Ordinary Core/Bridge restart, persistent SQLite/spool, and
subsequent acquisition passed. Separating Bridge handshake wait from command
timeout and hardening replacement-session preemption remain future reliability
work.

## Scope and operating claim

V1 is suitable for supervised laboratory acquisition under the documented
power, hardware, and operator gates. It is not a production-ready unattended
system, does not provide external-network security hardening, and does not
predict SOC/SOH or diagnose internal electrochemical mechanisms by itself.
